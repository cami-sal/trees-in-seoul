Scent Auras & Colors Guide · 향기 오라와 색상 원리

How botanical tree scents and ambient auras are visualised on the map
1
Center Core Dot (가로수 대표 위치)

An illustrative representative location for visualization purposes (based on official district tree totals, not individual GPS coordinates).

2
Diffused Color Aura (공기 중 향기 구름)

The ambient olfactory cloud that blends into the neighborhood atmosphere. The color gradient identifies the botanical tree species and its fragrance dispersion.

🎨 Why do trees have different aura colors? (수종별 향기 색상의 차이)
  • Concentric Aura (Top 2 Species): In the default city view, the aura highlights the top two most abundant tree species in each district — the 2nd highest species is placed in the center/middle core, while the #1 most abundant species forms the outer surrounding layer.
  • Percentage View: Switches to a multi-color breakdown where each color slice angle strictly equals that species' percentage of trees.
  • Botanical Identity: Each species has a unique color reflecting its olfactory character (e.g. amber gold for autumn ginkgo fruit, rose pink for spring cherry blossoms, cyan for evergreen pine phytoncides).

Botanical Species Scent Palette (수종별 향기 색상)

Ginkgo (은행나무) — Autumn 🍂 Pungent & rancid aroma from fallen seeds in autumn streets.
Yoshino Cherry (왕벚나무) — Spring 🌸 Sweet, delicate, refreshing floral aroma during spring blooming.
Fringe Tree (이팝나무) — Spring 🌸 Honey-like fragrance rich in phenylethyl alcohol volatile oils.
Korean Red Pine (소나무) — Evergreen 🌲 Crisp, resinous, invigorating phytoncide emitted year-round.
Plane Tree (양버즘나무) — Summer ☀️ Aromatic green foliage with fresh summer canopy leaf oils.
Zelkova (느티나무) — Summer ☀️ Subtle, grounding herbal and green wood aroma throughout summer.
Pagoda Tree (회화나무) — Summer ☀️ Distinctly fragrant, sweet creamy-white blossom notes.
Horse Chestnut (칠엽수) — Spring 🌸 Mild sweet floral fragrance from large white flower clusters.
Dawn Redwood (메타세콰이어) — Summer ☀️ Mild conifer scent with fresh resinous phytoncides in towering avenues.
Rose of Sharon (무궁화) — Summer ☀️ Faint, delicate, elegant floral scent from Korean national blossom.
Other Street Trees (기타 수종) — Mixed 🌿 Diverse urban canopy mix combining subtle native and ornamental tree aromas.
